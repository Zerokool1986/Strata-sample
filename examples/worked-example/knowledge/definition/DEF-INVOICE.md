# DEF-INVOICE — An invoice is a request for payment, not a receipt

## Summary

In Ledgerline, an *invoice* is the document that requests payment for work
already agreed. A *receipt* is a separate document issued after payment
arrives. The two are never the same record.

## The definition

- An invoice is created by the freelancer, sent to the client, and is immutable
  once sent. Corrections are handled by a credit note or a new invoice linked
  to the original — never by editing the sent invoice.
- A receipt is generated by Ledgerline when a payment is recorded against an
  invoice. It states what was paid, when, and against which invoice.

## What it includes / excludes

Includes: itemised work, amounts, tax fields as entered, due date (see
DEF-OVERDUE-30). Excludes: proof of payment (that is the receipt's job) and
the reminder history (that lives on the invoice record as events, not in the
document).

## Why this entry exists

Early support mail showed freelancers sending "the invoice" to clients after
payment as proof, and clients treating a resent invoice as a second bill.
The confusion caused one real duplicate payment. Settled by decision of the
project owner, 2026-01-12.
