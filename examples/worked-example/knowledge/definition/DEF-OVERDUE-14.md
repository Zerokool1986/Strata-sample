# DEF-OVERDUE-14 — (SUPERSEDED) An invoice is overdue 14 days after sending

> This entry is **superseded** by DEF-OVERDUE-30. It is kept, unedited, as the
> historical record of the earlier definition. Do not use it.

## The definition (as originally written)

An invoice counts as overdue, and enters the reminder schedule as overdue, 14
days after the date it was sent.

## Why it was replaced

The 14-day rule was set at project start by assumption, before any payment
data existed. Once OBS-LATE-PAYMENTS showed a median payment time of 23 days,
a 14-day overdue line classified most normal invoices as late, and the
reminder schedule built on it nagged clients who were behaving normally.
Superseded 2026-03-04; see DEF-OVERDUE-30. Note that the help page still
describes this 14-day rule — that disagreement is recorded in
CON-PAYMENT-TERMS.
